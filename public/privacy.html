Privacy Policy

League Predictions · last updated September 2026

League Predictions is a small, non-commercial game where friends predict the final tables of six football leagues and compare their guesses with the real standings. This page explains what information the site uses and why.

What we collect

When you sign in with Google, we receive from Google:

  • your name,
  • your email address, and
  • your profile picture.

We do not receive your Google password, and we do not access your contacts, calendar, files or any other Google data.

While you use the site we also store what you create in it: the tables you predict, the favorites you mark, and any request you send to link yourself to a past player. The site owner may also enter predictions of past players who never had an account, under the name used for them at the time.

How it is used

  • Your name and picture identify you to yourself and to other players, for example on the leaderboard.
  • Your email address is used to recognise your account. It is only visible to the site owner.
  • Your predictions are used to score you against the real standings.

We do not sell your information, do not show advertising, and do not use analytics or tracking tools.

Who can see it

Other players can see your name, picture and predictions. Predictions stay hidden from everyone else until the first match of the league season has been played, so nobody can copy them. Your email address and your favorites are not shown to other players.

Where it is stored and who processes it

The site relies on a few service providers that process information on its behalf:

  • Google, to sign you in;
  • Supabase, which hosts the database and the sign-in system;
  • Vercel, which hosts the website.

Football results and standings come from football-data.org. No personal information is sent to it.

Cookies and local storage

The site keeps your sign-in session in your browser so you stay signed in, and remembers small preferences such as the last league and leaderboard view you looked at. It uses nothing for advertising or tracking.

Your choices

You can sign out at any time from the menu. You can ask for your account and your predictions to be deleted, or for your name to be changed, by contacting the person who runs the site (the person who invited you). Deleting your account removes your predictions and favorites.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will be updated with a new date.
